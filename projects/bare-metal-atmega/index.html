Bare-Metal LED on ATmega328P

Register-level C firmware to drive GPIO and blink LEDs on the ATmega328P.

Small, dependency-free firmware targeting the AVR toolchain.

  • Used avr-gcc and avrdude with Makefile automation for quick compile/flash cycles.
  • Manipulated GPIO registers directly and cross-checked against datasheets and schematics.
